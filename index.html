About

I am a physician and clinical researcher with a background in infectious and tropical diseases, clinical epidemiology, vaccine research, and patient safety. I hold an MD, a specialist qualification in infectious and tropical diseases, and a PhD in Clinical Medicine from the University of Copenhagen.

My research has involved observational cohorts, registry-based studies, vaccine immunogenicity and safety, and quantitative analysis of clinical data. I have worked with multidisciplinary research teams in Denmark and internationally.

I am extending this experience into clinical AI evaluation, with particular interest in identifying clinically significant errors, assessing evidence reliability, and evaluating the safety of AI-generated medical information.

My analytical background includes advanced R programming, statistical modelling, and critical appraisal of scientific literature. I am also developing Python skills for reproducible AI evaluation workflows.

Clinical AI Evaluation

ClariSafe — Clinical AI Evaluation Portfolio

ClariSafe is an independent portfolio project focused on the clinical accuracy, patient safety, and evidence reliability of AI-generated medical responses.

The project demonstrates structured clinical review, identification and severity grading of medical errors, reference verification, and documentation of evaluation outcomes. It includes illustrative cases and evaluations of actual AI-model responses, with Python scripts for validating structured evaluation records.

Research

Lipid trajectories and multi-omics in HIV

My recent research collaboration at CHIP, Rigshospitalet, focuses on longitudinal lipid profiles and cardiometabolic risk in people with HIV, including group-based trajectory modelling and the integration of clinical and omics data.

Vaccine immunogenicity and safety

My research includes an ENFORCE cohort study examining the immunogenicity and safety of coadministered COVID-19, influenza, and pneumococcal vaccines, published in Clinical Infectious Diseases. I also contributed to Danish vaccination guidance for adult solid organ transplant recipients.

Drug safety and evidence synthesis

I have conducted systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and aggregate safety assessments, with a focus on interpreting clinical evidence and evaluating the safety of medical interventions.

Registry-based and cohort research

My research experience includes ENFORCE, PERSIMUNE, MATCH, and Danish registry data, with applications in infectious diseases, vaccination, and clinical outcomes.
