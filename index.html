About

I am an infectious diseases specialist and clinical researcher. I trained and worked as a doctor in Iran from 2010 to 2019, then moved to Denmark and completed a PhD in clinical medicine at the University of Copenhagen in 2023.

My work uses Danish registries and cohorts to study vaccines, infections in transplant recipients, and drug safety. I now work with the CHIP multi-omics team at Rigshospitalet on blood lipids and cardiometabolic risk in people with HIV.

I have 65 publications, 34 of them original articles (August 2026). I use R for most analyses.

Research

Lipids and multi-omics in HIV

At CHIP I study dyslipidemia in people with HIV using genomic, proteomic and lipidomic data from several European countries. The methods are clustering and group-based trajectory modelling.

Vaccine safety and effectiveness

I am first author of an ENFORCE sub-study on giving COVID-19, influenza and PPSV23 vaccines together (Clinical Infectious Diseases, 2026). I also co-wrote the Danish national vaccination guideline for adult solid organ transplant recipients.

Drug safety and aggregate safety studies

I have designed and led aggregate safety studies. These pool data from published articles and analyse them together, and they include benefit-risk evaluation. I presented three safety posters at ESCMID Global 2025.

Registry and cohort research

I work with ENFORCE, PERSIMUNE, MATCH and Statistics Denmark data. I am also a senior collaborator in the Global Burden of Disease study.
