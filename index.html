About

I am a clinical researcher and physician by training, with a PhD in Clinical Medicine from the University of Copenhagen and specialist training in infectious and tropical diseases. My experience spans clinical epidemiology, vaccine research, drug safety, and evidence synthesis.

I am applying this background to clinical AI evaluation, focusing on medical accuracy, patient safety, and the reliability of AI-generated evidence. My work combines clinical reasoning, critical appraisal of scientific literature, and quantitative research, including advanced statistical analysis in R.

Clinical AI Evaluation

ClariSafe — Clinical AI Safety & Evaluation

ClariSafe is an independent portfolio project demonstrating structured evaluation of AI-generated medical responses, with a focus on clinical accuracy, patient safety, and evidence reliability.

The project includes evaluations of clinical reasoning, potentially harmful recommendations, and supporting medical references. Findings are documented using defined error categories, severity levels, and evaluation outcomes.

The portfolio contains illustrative safety cases and a documented evaluation of a real AI-model response. Structured evaluation records are checked using Python validation scripts and automated tests.

Research

HIV and cardiometabolic research

My recent research collaboration at CHIP, Rigshospitalet, has focused on longitudinal lipid profiles and cardiometabolic risk in people with HIV, including group-based trajectory modelling and multi-omics research.

Vaccine immunogenicity and safety

My research includes an ENFORCE cohort study investigating the immunogenicity and safety of coadministered COVID-19, influenza, and pneumococcal vaccines, published in Clinical Infectious Diseases.

I have also contributed to national vaccination guidance for adult solid organ transplant recipients.

Drug safety and evidence synthesis

I have conducted systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and aggregate safety studies, with an emphasis on clinical interpretation, methodological quality, and benefit-risk assessment.

Clinical epidemiology

My experience includes analyses using ENFORCE, PERSIMUNE, MATCH, and Danish registry data, investigating infectious diseases, vaccination, and clinical outcomes.
